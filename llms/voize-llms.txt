# voize

> voize (voize GmbH) is a German healthcare AI company building a voice-documentation
> platform for long-term care, skilled nursing, and disability-support facilities.
> Caregivers speak naturally on a smartphone during patient care and voize's speech
> models convert the free speech into structured, categorized entries that sync
> bi-directionally in real time into the facility's electronic health record (EHR).
> The product works offline, recognizes patient names, and integrates with major EHRs.
> voize ships a mobile application; it does not currently publish a public developer API.
